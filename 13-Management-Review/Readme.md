# Management Review

## Purpose

This folder contains the first management review of the BlueSky Technologies ISMS,
held in line with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 9.3.

The review considers the Clause 9.3.2 inputs documented in
[`Management-Review-Agenda.md`](Management-Review-Agenda.md) and records the Clause 9.3.3 results, including
risk-owner decisions, improvement decisions, ISMS changes and resulting actions.

## Limitations

- **Interim evidence:** the ITGC results presented are Q1 2026 interim results and do
  not establish full-year operating effectiveness.
- **Evidence cut-off:** the decisions in this folder were made after the internal audit
  report dated 22 May 2026. They are follow-up evidence and do not change the audit
  conclusion as of that date.
- **Role-based record:** attendance and decisions are recorded by role. The folder
  contains no signatures or separate approval records.
- **Objectives:** information security objectives (Clause 6.2) were not audited, so
  their absence is recorded as a management decision and action, not as an audit
  finding.
- **Due dates:** the due dates in the tracker were set at the meeting date and have not
  been updated since.
